Create a 3D concept of a two-handed gaming controller.
Use millimetres, with the controls facing upward along +Z.

Main body:
- A rounded central body, 140 mm wide, 70 mm deep and 20 mm thick.
- Round the vertical corners with a 6 mm radius.
- Add two rounded grips extending approximately 40 mm backward.
- Use elliptical lofts for the grips and attach them to the body.

Controls on the top surface:
- Two analogue joysticks, positioned symmetrically near the lower centre.
  Each should have a 6 mm radius stem and a 10 mm radius thumb cap.
- A raised cross-shaped directional pad on the upper left,
  approximately 20 mm across and 3 mm tall.
- Four circular face buttons on the upper right, arranged in a diamond.
  Each button should be 9 mm in diameter and 3 mm tall,
  with 12 mm spacing between neighbouring button centres.

Keep the body, each grip, each joystick, the directional pad
and each face button as separate selectable components.
Place their connecting surfaces in contact and avoid unintended overlaps.

Use a dark grey body, black grips and joysticks, and green,
red, blue and yellow face buttons.

Make the dimensions editable through named parameters.
Preserve each component's construction history.
Focus on the exterior form and visible controls.
